MANGALORE REFINERY AND PETROCHEMICALS LIMITED (MRPL)
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: PUMP INSPECTION & INTEGRITY MONITORING
Document ID: MRPL-SOP-ENG-042 | Revision: 4.2 | Classification: RESTRICTED
================================================================================

1. OBJECTIVE & SCOPE
This standard operating procedure governs the periodic mechanical integrity,
NDT testing, and vibration baseline evaluation of API 610 centrifugal process
pumps deployed in CDU (Crude Distillation Unit) and VDU processing units.

2. MANDATORY PRE-REQUISITES
- Hazardous Work Permit (Class A Hot/Cold) authorized by Safety Officer.
- Complete mechanical lock-out/tag-out (LOTO) of motor 3.3kV feeder breaker.
- Depressurization and chemical neutralization of pump casing to 0.0 barg.
- Continuous LEL (Lower Explosive Limit) monitoring showing 0.0% combustible gas.

3. VIBRATION CRITERIA (ISO 10816-3 CLASS I/II RIGID MOUNT)
- Zone A (New / Overhauled): Overall velocity RMS < 2.3 mm/s
- Zone B (Unrestricted Long-Term Operation): 2.3 mm/s to 4.5 mm/s RMS
- Zone C (Alarm / Investigation Trigger): 4.5 mm/s to 7.1 mm/s RMS
- Zone D (Immediate Trip & Shutdown): > 7.1 mm/s RMS

4. BEARING TEMPERATURE LIMITS
- Hydrodynamic Sleeve Bearings: Maximum 85°C (185°F)
- Antifriction Rolling Bearings: Maximum 93°C (200°F)
- Lube oil supply temperature must remain between 40°C and 49°C.

5. NON-DESTRUCTIVE TESTING (NDT) WALL THICKNESS
- Suction nozzle nominal thickness: 18.0 mm (Corrosion Allowance: 3.0 mm)
- Minimum retirement wall thickness (MRWT): 14.5 mm
- If ultrasonic reading falls below 15.0 mm, ultrasonic B-scan grid is mandatory.
